    About PrimeNest

    PrimeNest started in 2021 as a two-person outfit helping relatives find flats in Lagos without going through five different agents. It grew the way word-of-mouth grows: slowly, then all at once. We now work across five cities, but we've kept the habit that started it — one agent sees a deal through from first viewing to signed keys.

    We don't list everything. If a property wouldn't pass our own inspection, it doesn't go up.

    How we work

    Every listing is inspected in person. Prices are checked against three comparable sales before we publish. No property stays listed past 90 days without a review.
